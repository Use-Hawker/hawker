Deploy the HAWKER till

Pay-per-call settlement in USDG on Robinhood Chain: a buyer signs a payment for one call, the till moves the USDG from the buyer to the seller and takes the fee, all in one transaction. It holds nothing in between. The owner can only change the fee (never above 5%), the fee wallet and the token that waives the fee. It cannot move anyone's USDG.
